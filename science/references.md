# LABMATE — Scientific references

**Experiment:** Sandwich ELISA for human C-Reactive Protein (CRP), 96-well plate  
**Main protocol:** R&D Systems Human CRP DuoSet ELISA (DY1707)  
**Sources accessed:** 2026-11-08  

* **`ref_01`** — Human C-Reactive Protein/CRP DuoSet ELISA Development System, Catalog # DY1707 (product datasheet), R&D Systems / Bio-Techne, datasheet rev. 10/26  
  https://resources.rndsystems.com/pdfs/datasheets/dy1707.pdf  
  **Used for:** all assay steps (coat, block, sample/standard, detection antibody, Streptavidin-HRP, substrate, stop, read); volumes; incubation times and temperatures; wash procedure (3 x 400 µL Wash Buffer); buffer compositions (Reagent Diluent/Block Buffer = 1% BSA in PBS; Wash Buffer = 0.05% Tween 20 in PBS); 7-point 2-fold standard curve; "assay all standards and samples in duplicate"; calculation (average duplicates, subtract zero standard, 4-PL curve fit, multiply by dilution factor); 450 nm read with 540/570 nm correction.  
  **Note:** antibody working concentrations and high-standard concentration are lot-specific (Certificate of Analysis) and are not stated in the datasheet text.

* **`ref_02`** — Human C-Reactive Protein/CRP DuoSet ELISA, product page (Product Details + Procedure), R&D Systems  
  https://rndsystems.com/products/human-c-reactive-protein-crp-duoset-elisa_dy1707  
  **Used for:** assay range 15.6–1,000 pg/mL; sample volume 100 µL; 96-well strip plate format; TMB substrate, 450 nm colorimetric detection; warning that uncorrected 450 nm readings may be higher and less accurate.

* **`ref_03`** — How to Run an R&D Systems DuoSet ELISA (protocol guide), R&D Systems  
  https://www.rndsystems.com/resources/protocols/how-run-rd-systems-duoset-elisa  
  **Used for:** example plate layout (standard curve in duplicate in strips 1–2, samples in duplicate in strips 3–6, remaining wells blanks); standard curve preparation order (prepare standards last); worked 1000 → 500 → 250 pg/mL serial dilution example.

* **`ref_04`** — ELISA Troubleshooting Guide, R&D Systems  
  https://www.rndsystems.com/resources/protocols/elisa-troubleshooting-guide  
  **Used for:** constraint "no bubbles in wells before reading" — lists "Plate contains bubbles, affecting the optical reading" as a cause of high variability between replicates (remedy: centrifuge plate before reading); constraint "read immediately after stop solution".

* **`ref_05`** — Inconsistent results and high CV in ELISA (troubleshooting), Abcam  
  https://www.abcam.com/en-us/technical-resources/troubleshooting/inconsistent-results-and-high-cv-elisa  
  **Used for:** constraint "no bubbles in wells before reading" (second, independent source — ensure no bubbles before reading; pop with a dry pipette tip; add solutions gently); duplicate CV acceptance target (< 15%).
